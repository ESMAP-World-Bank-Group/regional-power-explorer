1 Tool Overview

The Regional Power Explorer is an interactive web tool visualising electricity sector data across ~16 regional power pools and interconnected zones, covering 161 countries. It serves EPM teams doing power sector work and is intended to support ESMAP regional engagement. The tool is currently in development (not publicly released).

4F — Trade & Cross-Border Integration

Trade is the single most important indicator for a regional power tool — it is what makes a "pool" a pool. Coverage is currently 76 / 161 countries with bilateral import/export flows (GWh by partner, by year), shown as a diverging bar chart with a net-balance line on each country's Supply & Trade tab. A further 12 countries are documented as having no cross-border flows — isolated island/archipelago grids (Cyprus, Madagascar, Cabo Verde, Brunei, Philippines, Angola) or systems with no integrated grid (South Sudan, Somalia, Somaliland, Yemen), plus jointly-reported zones (Luxembourg in DE-LU, Kosovo in the Serbian block) — shown as a clear note rather than a blank chart. Sources are mixed by nature and must always be labelled: ENTSO-E physical flows (high), regional secretariat statistics (high), national TSO data (high), and UN Comtrade HS 2716 customs declarations (medium — patchy, often zero-filled).

Every country chart now states what it shows, its source, and a reliability tag (Official / Estimated / Partial) directly beneath it — applied to both the supply and the trade charts so the reader never has to guess the provenance of a number.
